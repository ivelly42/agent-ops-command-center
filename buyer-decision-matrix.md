| Hourly Value | Cleanup Time Avoided | Value Recovered | Decision Signal |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `$30/hour` | 30 minutes | `$15` | Not enough alone |
| `$30/hour` | 1 hour | `$30` | Break-even |
| `$50/hour` | 1 hour | `$50` | Clear yes |
| `$50/hour` | 4 hours | `$200` | Strong yes |

## Buy If

- One avoided cleanup session is worth more than `$29`.
- You run multiple AI-agent sessions per week.
- You need durable proof for done claims, release gates, PR reviews, or revenue claims.
- You want Notion / CSV structure without designing the workspace from scratch.

## Do Not Buy If

- You run one-off experiments and do not care about evidence.
- You already have a maintained agent-run database.
- You need custom implementation rather than templates.
- You expect payment, legal, tax, payout, or account setup help.

## Revenue Rule

Revenue is proven only by checkout, receipt, payout, or seller-dashboard evidence.

Purchase-intent issues, stars, downloads, comments, Gist views, and private intent are not revenue.
